# D6.3 acceptance criteria

**EXP-01** The explorer filters every published activity row by compound (InChIKey prefix or SMILES fragment), assay type, variant, source and pIC50 range, with flagged rows kept, labelled and optionally hidden, and the page's own script is tested to narrow the rows correctly.
**EXP-02** Every row links to its source record: the ChEMBL activity and assay, or the PubChem bioassay and substance parsed from the record id, and the page carries the snapshot's licence and attribution.
